Know Your Rights

Understanding your legal rights is the first step toward protection and justice

Equality before law

No person is above the law — whether rich or poor, powerful or ordinary, everyone is governed by the same legal principles.

Freedom from Discrimination

No discrimination based on religion, race, caste, sex, or place of birth in public places and employment.

Equality of opportunity in public employment

Equal access to government jobs and public services without any discrimination.

Abolition of untouchability

Prohibition of the practice of untouchability in any form, making it a punishable offense.

Abolition of titles

No titles shall be conferred by the State, except military and academic distinctions.

Freedom of speech and expression

Right to freely express opinions and ideas without censorship, subject to reasonable restrictions.

Freedom to assemble peacefully

Right to assemble peacefully without arms for lawful purposes.

Freedom to form associations/unions

Right to form associations, unions, or cooperative societies.

Freedom to move freely within India

Right to move freely throughout the territory of India.

Freedom to reside anywhere in India

Right to reside and settle in any part of the country.

Freedom to practice any profession

Right to practice any profession, or to carry on any occupation, trade or business.

Protection of life and personal liberty (Article 21)

No person shall be deprived of his life or personal liberty except according to procedure established by law.

Rights of arrested persons

Protection against arrest and detention in certain cases, including right to be informed of grounds of arrest.

Prohibition of human trafficking

Complete ban on trafficking in human beings and forced labor.

Prohibition of forced labor

No person can be forced to provide labor without payment or under compulsion.

Prohibition of child labor

Children below 14 years cannot be employed in factories, mines, or hazardous occupations.

Freedom of conscience and religion

Freedom to profess, practice, and propagate any religion according to one's conscience.

Freedom to manage religious affairs

Right to establish and maintain religious institutions and manage religious affairs.

No compulsory religious instruction

No religious instruction shall be provided in state-funded educational institutions.

Freedom from religious taxes

No person shall be compelled to pay taxes for promotion of any particular religion.

Protection of language and culture

Right of any group of citizens to conserve their distinct language, script, or culture.

Right of minorities to establish educational institutions

Minorities have the right to establish and administer educational institutions of their choice.

No discrimination in educational institutions

No citizen shall be denied admission to any educational institution maintained by the State on grounds only of religion, race, caste, language or any of them.

Right to approach Supreme Court

The right to move the Supreme Court for enforcement of fundamental rights through appropriate proceedings.

Power of Supreme Court to issue writs

Supreme Court can issue directions, orders or writs including habeas corpus, mandamus, prohibition, quo warranto and certiorari.

Suspension of rights during emergency

This right cannot be suspended except as provided by the Constitution during emergency.

Dr. B. R. Ambedkar's view

Called this the "heart and soul" of the Constitution as it makes other fundamental rights meaningful and enforceable.

Recognition as fundamental right

Recognized as a fundamental right by Supreme Court in 2017 under Article 21 (Right to Life and Personal Liberty).

Protection of personal data

Right to protect personal information, data privacy, and control over personal data.

Bodily autonomy and private life

Protection of bodily autonomy, personal choices, and private life from state and non-state interference.

Digital privacy

Extension of privacy rights to digital spaces, protecting online activities and digital communications.

Right to Digital Privacy

Your personal data cannot be collected or processed without your explicit consent. Organizations must clearly state how they use your data and obtain your permission before collecting it.

Right to Be Forgotten

You can request the removal of your personal data from online platforms and search engines when the data is no longer necessary or you withdraw your consent.

Right to Data Portability

You have the right to receive your personal data in a structured, commonly used format and transfer it to another service provider without hindrance.

Protection from Cyber Crimes

Legal protection against hacking, phishing, identity theft, online fraud, and other cyber crimes. Authorities are obligated to investigate and prosecute such offenses.

Right to Digital Access

Equal access to digital services and information, regardless of disability, location, or socioeconomic status. Service providers must ensure accessibility for all users.

Right to Safety

Protection against hazardous goods, services, and unfair trade practices. Products must meet safety standards and not pose risks to health or property.

Right to Information

Complete and accurate information about products and services, including price, quality, potency, purity, and standard. No misleading advertisements or false claims.

Right to Choose

Access to a variety of goods and services at competitive prices. Freedom to select from multiple options without coercion or restrictive trade practices.

Right to Redressal

Fair settlement of genuine grievances through consumer courts and forums. Compensation for unsatisfactory services or defective products.

Right to Consumer Education

Knowledge about consumer rights and responsibilities. Awareness programs to empower consumers to make informed decisions.

Right to Fair Banking

Transparent banking practices, clear terms and conditions, and fair treatment from financial institutions. Protection from hidden fees and unfair charges.

Right to Credit Information

Access to your credit reports and scores. Right to dispute inaccurate information and have it corrected in a timely manner.

Right to Financial Privacy

Confidentiality of your financial transactions and account information. Banks cannot disclose your financial details without legal requirement or your consent.

Right to Grievance Redressal

Access to banking ombudsman and other dispute resolution mechanisms for timely resolution of complaints against financial institutions.

Protection from Fraud

Banks and financial institutions are responsible for implementing security measures to protect customers from fraud and unauthorized transactions.
